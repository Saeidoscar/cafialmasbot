Contact Exporter

This is the real extension popup, running against mock data so you can click through it. Pick a web app to see its state, then press Scan contacts — the simulated scan streams progress and fills the list.

In the real extension this popup runs inside your logged-in Telegram / WhatsApp / Rubika / Eitaa tab. It scrolls the contact list, harvests each row, and stores everything locally — no server, no password, no API key.
  • Scan contacts — automatic row detection + scrolling until the list ends.
  • Teach me — click one row and the repeating pattern is learned (works even after a redesign).
  • Diagnose — lists the repeating selectors found on the page.
  • Export as CSV, vCard .vcf, Google Contacts CSV, JSON.
